Real refraction — liquid glass (drag-free preview)

In June last year Apple announced Liquid Glass at WWDC, a fresh design language built around the physics of light bending through curved glass. The content renders normally and stays visible everywhere outside the lens — the whole idea is that it refracts live HTML, the real rendered content. Because it works on the real rendered content rather than a copy of it, the glass can go on anything made of live DOM: text, images, cards, buttons, whole layouts.
