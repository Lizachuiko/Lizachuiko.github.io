Privacy Policy — Pearl

Last updated: 7 June 2026

Pearl is a Chrome extension that helps you edit, translate, and generate text inside input fields on the web using AI. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

What data is processed

  • Text you choose to process. When you click an action (Edit, Translate, Generate, etc.), the text in the focused field — or the text you selected — is sent for processing so the AI can return a result.
  • Your writing-style note (optional). If you fill in the "Writing Style" field in the extension settings, that short note is sent along with your requests to guide the output.

What is NOT collected

  • Pearl does not read or send any text unless you explicitly trigger an action.
  • Pearl does not collect your browsing history, passwords, cookies, or personal identifiers.
  • Pearl does not show ads or sell data to third parties.

How requests are handled

Requests are sent over HTTPS to our own proxy server hosted on Cloudflare Workers. The proxy forwards the text to the AI provider (Anthropic, Claude API) to generate a response, then returns the result to the extension. The text is used only to produce your result and is not stored by Pearl after the request completes.

Third-party processing

Text you submit is processed by Anthropic to generate responses, subject to Anthropic's privacy and usage policies. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Local storage

Your optional writing-style note is stored using Chrome's storage.sync so it persists across your signed-in browsers. You can clear it any time from the extension settings.
